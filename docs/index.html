Free, with no catch

The app costs nothing, shows no ads and sells nothing inside, and there's no account to make. The lists ship inside the app, so they open right away and keep working when the signal drops out in the canyons.

What stays on your phone

Beehive Eats collects nothing about you: no sign-in, no analytics, no ads. Location, if you turn it on, only puts the nearest places first, and your saved places are kept on the phone or iPad. The website has no cookies and no trackers. The privacy policy has the details.

Questions

Is Beehive Eats free?

Completely: no price, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to create. It's an iPhone and iPad app on its way to the App Store, with no Android version yet; until it's out, the web search here works in any browser.

Where do the lists come from?

Two kinds of source. The Salt Lake classics, red rock country, pastrami burger, Pacific Islander, Park City, pie and oldest-places lists are our own research from fall 2026, with a 2025 or 2026 source or an official tourism listing behind each place. The wider directory of 6,168 restaurants starts from Overture Maps' open place data and keeps the kinds of listing that proved reliable in the states that publish official restaurant lists; Utah publishes none.

Where can I get a pastrami burger?

Our pastrami burger list has 5 hand-checked places in the Salt Lake Valley, from Crown Burgers downtown, where the chain began in 1978, to Yanni's Greek Express in Sugar House. In the web search, the list puts the nearest first once you share your location.

How do I check a Utah restaurant's inspection?

Each of Utah's 13 local health departments inspects its own restaurants and publishes its reports only in its own search, and none posts a letter grade. So the app shows no inspection results: each place links to the list of health departments, and we compute no grade.

Does it show which places serve alcohol?

No. Utah's Department of Alcoholic Beverage Services licenses the bars and restaurants that pour alcohol, but publishes no list we may read, so the app doesn't say either way.

Does it show ratings and reviews?

Not from us. Tapping a place opens Apple Maps' own card inside the app, with Apple's current ratings, hours, photos and directions. None of our lists is sorted by anyone's rating.

Does it need my location?

No. Location only matters for putting the nearest places first. If you share it, it stays on your iPhone or iPad and never reaches us, and the app works fine without it. On this website, Near me reads your location inside your browser and nowhere else.

A place has closed, or something's wrong. How do I tell you?

Write to nick@eatsranked.com with the place's name, its town and what changed. Corrections ship with the next data update.
