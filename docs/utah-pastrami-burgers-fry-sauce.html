Pastrami burgers & fry sauce

5 places for the Utah pastrami burger: a cheeseburger under a pile of hot pastrami, with fry sauce on the side. Each was checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Where it came from

The Salt Lake Tribune (March 2025) traces Utah's pastrami burger to a handful of family burger stands: Omega Burger brought it to Utah in 1977, Crown Burgers opened downtown in 1978, and Astro Burgers and Apollo Burger followed. Gastronomic SLC's 2026 burger guide adds Yanni's Greek Express in Sugar House and Golden Gyros, whose house burger comes with gyro meat and fry sauce.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Salt Lake City (4), Millcreek (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
