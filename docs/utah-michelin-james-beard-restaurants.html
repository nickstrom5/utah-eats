Utah MICHELIN & James Beard restaurants

20 Utah restaurants recognized by MICHELIN or the James Beard Foundation. The MICHELIN Guide Southwest's first selection lists 9 Utah places we could place (one 1-Star, three Bib Gourmand and five Recommended restaurants); 11 places hold a James Beard honor (3 finalists and 8 semifinalists).

What counts as an honor here

Two sources, both reported as facts rather than scores. One is the MICHELIN Guide Southwest, whose first selection was revealed in Las Vegas in August 2026, with a star for Monte in South Salt Lake. The other is the James Beard Foundation: its 2025 and 2026 nominees and semifinalists. In 2026 Travis Herbert of Felt Bar & Eatery and Nick Zocco of Urban Hill were Best Chef: Mountain nominees. Where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place says so. Reader polls, "best of" roundups and review-site stars don't count.
