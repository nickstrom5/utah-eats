Red rock country: where to eat near Utah's national parks

21 places to eat in the gateway towns of Utah's red rock country: Moab, Green River, Torrey, Boulder, Escalante, Tropic, Panguitch and Kanab. Each is on Visit Utah's dining guides, checked in fall 2026.

Ask for early accessThe free Beehive Eats app for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

Between the parks

Visit Utah's dining guide to the towns between the national parks and its foodie itinerary name these places. Hell's Backbone Grill in Boulder grows almost everything on its menu; Capitol Burger in Torrey is a food truck known for its mac 'n cheese burger. Many of these kitchens keep seasons, so a dark window in winter is seasonal, not closed.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Moab (7), Torrey (5), Boulder (3), Escalante (2), Panguitch (1), Kanab (1), Tropic (1), Green River (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (its own site or menu, a dated local news story or an official tourism listing) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Red-rock kitchens and ski-town tables keep seasons, and many close for part of the winter, so check before you drive.
